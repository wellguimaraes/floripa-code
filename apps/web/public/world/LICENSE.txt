character.glb is the "Rogue" from KayKit : Adventurers Character Pack (1.0)
by Kay Lousberg (www.kaylousberg.com), trimmed to the animations the world
view uses, without its props, and with a grayscale texture.

Source: https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0
License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0), http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

nature.glb holds small rocks and grass from KayKit : Forest Nature
Pack (1.0), free tier, by Kay Lousberg (www.kaylousberg.com): the models the
world view uses, merged into one file without materials or the texture. The
texture's gradient bands live on as each vertex's UV, which the world recolours.

Source: https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-forest
License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0), http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

manager.glb is the "Ranger" from KayKit : Adventurers Character Pack (2.0),
free tier, by Kay Lousberg (www.kaylousberg.com), without his quiver, with
the Idle_A and Interact animations from the pack's Rig_Medium_General. It
keeps its own texture.

Source: https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-adventurers
License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0), http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

whale.glb is the "Whale" by Quaternius (quaternius.com), a humpback with a
Swim animation, as published on Poly Pizza. The world gives it its own colours.

Source: https://poly.pizza/m/JGFwp6xWgk
